// SECURITY

Your credentials.
Your machine.
Your control.

Automated trading requires access to your brokerage account. We take that responsibility seriously. FuturesForged is architected from the ground up so that your most sensitive data never has to leave your machine.

⚠ WHAT IS AT STAKE

Your Tradovate credentials — if compromised — grant direct access to your funded trading accounts. A bad actor with those credentials could place unauthorized trades, drain your evaluation account, or violate your prop firm's rules in ways that result in permanent termination.

This is not a theoretical risk. Credential theft is the leading cause of funded account loss outside of trading itself. Any software that requires your brokerage login must be held to an extremely high standard — and we hold ourselves to that standard every day.

✓ HOW WE PROTECT YOU
[01]
CREDENTIALS NEVER LEAVE YOUR MACHINE
Your Tradovate login is stored in a local .env file on your own computer. FuturesForged connects directly to Tradovate's servers — your credentials are never transmitted to or stored on any FuturesForged server.
[02]
DASHBOARD PASSWORD PROTECTION
The live dashboard is protected by a bcrypt-hashed password with session tokens. After 10 failed login attempts, the IP is locked out for 15 minutes. No one on your network can access your dashboard without your password.
[03]
WEBHOOK SIGNATURE VERIFICATION
TradingView webhooks are verified against a secret key using HMAC constant-time comparison. Requests from unknown IP addresses are rejected before any order logic is reached. Only verified signals execute trades.
[04]
NO TRADE DATA SENT TO OUR SERVERS
The only external call FuturesForged makes to our servers is a license key validation check. Your positions, P&L, account balances, and order history never leave your machine.
// ONGOING SECURITY COMMITMENT
ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
Security layers are added with every major release. We treat protection of your account access as a first-class engineering priority, not an afterthought.
LOCAL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE
FuturesForged is a desktop application by design. Cloud-based trading tools create a central point of failure. Local execution eliminates that risk entirely.
MACHINE-LOCKED LICENSING
Each license is cryptographically bound to a single machine. This prevents license sharing and ensures your software installation cannot be cloned or reused by others.
RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE
If you discover a security vulnerability in FuturesForged, contact us immediately. We take all reports seriously and commit to responding within 24 hours.
// FAQ

Common questions.

Is automated trading allowed by prop firms?
That depends entirely on the firm, and policies change without notice. Some firms permit automation, some restrict it to certain account stages, and some prohibit it outright — automated copy trading in particular is disallowed by a number of firms. FuturesForged does not use high-frequency trading and does not exploit simulation fills, but that is a statement about how the software behaves, not permission from your firm. Confirm your firm's current automation policy in writing before you enable any strategy. Compliance with your account agreement is your responsibility.
Which firms does this work with?
FuturesForged connects through Tradovate, so it works with any account you can reach that way. We do not claim approval, endorsement, or a partnership with any proprietary trading firm. Trailing floors, daily caps, consistency rules, and contract limits are values you enter for your own account and plan size, and every order is checked against them. Verify your firm's automation policy before going live.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. FuturesForged is a Windows desktop application. You download the installer, enter your Tradovate credentials, set your account's contract limits and plan size, and enable the strategies you want to run. The live dashboard opens in your browser automatically.
How does position sizing work?
FuturesForged reads your live account balance from Tradovate on each startup and scales contract size based on your current cushion to the trailing floor. It automatically switches from MNQ to NQ as your account grows, and back to MNQ if cushion gets thin. You never exceed the contract limits set by your prop firm.
Will it blow my funded account?
FuturesForged enforces your firm's exact rules automatically — it will never let you trade if you're within one stop loss of the floor. Emergency stop, daily loss limits, and intraday trailing floors are all applied in real time. No trading system eliminates risk, but the limits you configure are enforced more strictly than most human traders follow them manually.
What are the OOS profit factors based on?
All stated performance metrics are from out-of-sample walk-forward backtests on 2 years of Databento GLBX.MDP3 1-minute data. The IS/OOS split is 70/30. Past performance does not guarantee future results. These are backtested figures, not live trading results.
Can I run it on multiple accounts?
Yes. Pro supports multiple accounts, each with its own independent strategy toggles, position sizing, and prop rule enforcement. Desk adds a multi-account dashboard for managing them all from a single view.
What is the Strategy Generator?
Pro subscribers get access to an automated pattern discovery engine that tests over 1,150 parameter combinations on your historical data, walk-forward validates the results, and presents candidate strategies for review. New strategies require seller approval before they go live — they cannot self-deploy.
