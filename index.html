// FAQ

Common questions.

Which prop firms are supported?
MyFundedFutures, Apex Trader Funding, Topstep, TradeDay, and Earn2Trade are all pre-configured at launch. More firms are added regularly. All account rules — trailing floors, daily caps, consistency rules, and contract limits — are hardwired for each firm and plan size.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. FuturesForged is a Windows desktop application. You download the installer, enter your Tradovate credentials, select your prop firm and plan size, and enable the strategies you want to run. The live dashboard opens in your browser automatically.
How does position sizing work?
FuturesForged reads your live account balance from Tradovate on each startup and scales contract size based on your current cushion to the trailing floor. It automatically switches from MNQ to NQ as your account grows, and back to MNQ if cushion gets thin. You never exceed the contract limits set by your prop firm.
Will it blow my funded account?
FuturesForged enforces your firm's exact rules automatically — it will never let you trade if you're within one stop loss of the floor. Emergency stop, daily loss limits, and intraday trailing floors are all applied in real time. No trading system eliminates risk, but the prop firm rules are enforced more strictly than most human traders follow them manually.
What are the OOS profit factors based on?
All stated performance metrics are from out-of-sample walk-forward backtests on 2 years of Databento GLBX.MDP3 1-minute data. The IS/OOS split is 70/30. Past performance does not guarantee future results. These are backtested figures, not live trading results.
Can I run it on multiple accounts?
Starter supports up to 2 accounts. Pro supports unlimited accounts. Each account has its own independent strategy toggles, position sizing, and prop rule enforcement.
What is the Strategy Generator?
Pro subscribers get access to an automated pattern discovery engine that tests over 1,150 parameter combinations on your historical data, walk-forward validates the results, and presents candidate strategies for review. New strategies require seller approval before they go live — they cannot self-deploy.
