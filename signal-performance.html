Methodology

How these numbers are produced, and what they are not.

  • Rows come from the FuturesForged signals table, filtered to signals the engine has marked closed.
  • Entry and exit prices are the signal engine's own view of the market at signal and close time. They are not broker fill reports.
  • P&L is computed from those signal prices. It excludes commissions, exchange fees, and slippage, all of which would reduce real-world results.
  • Every figure is normalised to one micro contract per signal — $2.00 per point on NQ and MNQ, $5.00 per point on ES and MES. Signals on full-size contracts are reported at their micro equivalent, so these figures do not represent the size actually traded.
  • Legacy test rows that predate the signal reconciler are excluded from every figure on this page.
  • Where the engine did not record a close reason, the table shows — rather than an inferred value.
  • Broker-confirmed P&L is not yet published. When it is, it will appear as a separate, clearly labelled view — it will not replace this one.
